2. What the Service does

Agent Gateway connects your device with a paired agent, such as Meta Muse, to
request limited Gmail information. The app provides controls for authorizing
source access, reviewing results, and approving or denying disclosure. A relay
transports encrypted requests and results and supports notifications.
Muse can compose plain-text email and submit an encrypted send request through
the relay. Gateway displays the sender, To/Cc/Bcc recipients, subject, and full
message for Allow or Decline. Allow and device authentication submit the exact
reviewed message directly to Gmail. The app does not provide a compose editor,
create or
manage drafts in Gmail or support attachments.

Availability and supported features depend on your app version, device,
deployment configuration, and third-party services. Beta and TestFlight releases
may contain errors, change, or become unavailable.

3. Your accounts and approvals

Only connect accounts and disclose information you are authorized to access and
share. You are responsible for securing your device, account credentials, pairing
codes, and paired agents. Review pairing details, requested access, previews, and
any approval rules before accepting them. Remove pairings and revoke permissions
when they are no longer needed or you suspect unauthorized access.

Connecting Gmail requests Gmail read-only and send permissions, but does not
by itself approve agent requests or send any email. Preview retrieves a filtered
result for you to review
before a separate Allow or Deny decision. Allow on the initial request
authorizes retrieval and one-time sharing without displaying a preview first.
Deny on the initial request rejects it without retrieving email. The current
interface does not offer preview editing. Previously saved exact-scope approval
rules may permit disclosure until they expire or are reset, but do not bypass
source-access authorization or sensitive-field authentication.

Sending has a separate email review and Allow/Decline flow with device authentication.
Allow on a data request, a pairing approval, or a saved disclosure rule does
not authorize sending mail. You are responsible for checking the sender account,
all To, Cc, and Bcc recipients, and the exact subject and body before sending.
Closing a review does not decline or delete the stored request. The app does not recall sent
messages. If the result of a send is uncertain, check Gmail Sent before creating
another copy; the app does not automatically retry the attempted submission.
Existing read-only connections must grant send permission before using this flow.

Pairing can enable the app's notification setting by default. iOS permission
still controls notification delivery. Pairing, receiving an alert, or tapping
an alert does not itself approve a Gmail request.
