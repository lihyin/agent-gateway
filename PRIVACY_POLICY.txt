1. WHO WE ARE

Agent Gateway is operated by Zerobox ("we", "us"). It lets
you review requests from a paired Muse client for selected Gmail information
and decide what to share. This policy describes the iOS app and its associated
relay service. It does not replace the privacy policies of Google, Apple,
Cloudflare, Muse, or another service you choose to use.

Privacy contact: hello@zerobox.ai

This policy is available without signing in, on a dedicated web page linked
from our homepage, from the app's home screen, and before Google sign-in.

2. INFORMATION THE APP HANDLES

Gmail access. If you connect Gmail, Google handles your sign-in and consent.
Agent Gateway receives OAuth access and refresh tokens so it can make the
authorized Gmail API requests directly from your device. The app does not
receive or store your Google password. Gmail tokens stay in device-local
secure storage and are not sent to the relay or your paired agent.

The Google permissions requested are gmail.readonly and gmail.send:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send
gmail.readonly is a broad read-only Gmail permission. Although it can allow
reading mail, Agent Gateway limits its implemented access to the bounded
searches and selected fields described here. The Gmail API requires this
permission for the search queries used by the app; the narrower gmail.metadata
permission does not support those queries. The send permission allows sending
messages composed by Muse that you review and approve. The app does not request the broader
gmail.compose, gmail.modify, or full-mailbox permission, and does not manage
Gmail drafts or change or delete existing messages. We read your Gmail account's
email address through the Gmail profile endpoint to show the From address and
send from the connected account. This does not require Google account profile,
contacts, calendar, or Drive scopes. Existing read-only grants can keep reading;
allowing a Muse send request requires reconnecting to grant send permission.

Email information. Following source-access approval, the app can retrieve
bounded search results with selected fields such as message identifiers,
dates, senders, subjects, and short snippets. The current app does not retrieve
attachments or full message bodies from search results, and does not delete
or modify existing mail.
Search terms, request purposes, date ranges, and requested fields are processed
to prepare the review and enforce its scope.

Outgoing email. Your paired Muse client composes the From, To, Cc, and Bcc
addresses, subject, and plain-text message and submits an encrypted request
through the Cloudflare relay. Gateway displays the complete email for review.
Allow and device authentication are required before submitting it from iPhone to the
Gmail API. Gmail delivers it to the addressed recipients, including recipients
outside Google, and normally keeps a copy in Sent. These outgoing compositions
are known to Muse, which created them; the relay carries encrypted requests.
Muse receives the send status and Gmail message ID, not Gmail credentials.
The current send feature does not support attachments or server-side
Gmail draft management.

Pairing and security information. The app creates device keys and pairing
identifiers. The relay stores public device and agent pairing records,
encrypted notification destinations, update timestamps, rate-control records,
and revoked pairing identifiers. Device private keys remain on your device.
These records are used to authenticate requests, deliver notifications, prevent
replay and abuse, and honor pairing revocation.

Notification and delivery information. With notifications enabled, Apple Push
Notification service (APNs) handles a device push token and encrypted request
delivery. The relay processes encrypted requests and results. Notifications
use generic alert text rather than email content. The device temporarily
stores encrypted incoming requests until they are imported or expire.

Local review records. The app stores request state, proposed disclosures,
approval decisions, saved approval rules, and a limited local audit history
to support review, expiry, and replay protection.

Network information. Connections to Google, Apple, and the relay necessarily
expose network information such as IP addresses and connection timing to the
services handling those connections. Infrastructure providers may process
operational and security information under their own policies and settings.
The public website is also served through Cloudflare, which can process
connection and website performance information. This is separate from Gmail
information and does not give Cloudflare access to Gmail credentials or
decrypted email results.

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED AND SHARED

We use information to connect Gmail, pair your selected agent, deliver requests,
prepare previews, apply your decisions, return approved results, review and
send emails you approve, and protect the service against unauthorized use.

Connecting Gmail grants Google API permission; it does not by itself approve
a request from an agent. For a new request, Preview authorizes retrieval under
the request's limits and displays a filtered result for a separate Allow or
Deny decision. Allow on the initial request authorizes both retrieval and
one-time sharing of the filtered result without first displaying a preview.
Deny on the initial request rejects it without retrieving email. The current
interface does not offer editing of previews. Where a previously saved approval
rule applies, it is limited to its exact scope and expires within 24 hours;
source access still requires authorization. Additional device authentication
may be required for sensitive fields. Filtering is best effort and may miss
sensitive information. Choose Preview if you want to inspect data before sharing.

Allow on an agent data request never authorizes sending an email. Outgoing
email has a separate review, Allow/Decline, and device-authentication sequence
bound to the reviewed sender, recipients, subject, and message. Saved data
approval rules do not authorize sending. We do not send composed emails
automatically or in the background. If a send response is lost or uncertain,
the app asks you to check Sent rather than automatically retrying the message.

Notifications are enabled locally by default after confirmed pairing; iOS
notification permission still determines whether alerts are delivered. Pairing,
notification delivery, or tapping a notification does not on its own authorize
Gmail retrieval or disclosure.

Requests and results are encrypted between the paired endpoints. The relay
forwards encrypted content and does not have the endpoint private keys needed
to decrypt email previews or approved results. It does not have Gmail tokens
or authority to fetch your email or approve disclosure.

When you approve sharing, the paired Muse client receives and decrypts the
approved information to carry out your request. This is a transfer to the
recipient you paired. Review that service's privacy practices, account settings,
and retention policies before sharing. Agent Gateway cannot recall information
already delivered to another service.

Google provides Gmail authentication, email search, account sender information,
and email sending. Your addressed recipients receive the outgoing message and
may retain or forward it under their own practices. The app cannot recall sent
email or control copies in recipient mailboxes. Apple provides iOS
security services and push notifications. Cloudflare hosts the relay and its
pairing registry. These providers process the information necessary for those
functions and may process it in countries other than your own.

The app has no advertising SDK or third-party product-analytics SDK in its
current implementation. We do not sell Gmail information, use it for advertising,
or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning
models. Google Workspace API data, including derived data, is not used to
develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.
It is not used for credit decisions or lending, or provided to data brokers,
information resellers, or advertising platforms for those purposes.
Agent Gateway's use and transfer of information received from Google
APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google
Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including their Limited Use
requirements. User-directed sharing supports the requested user-facing feature;
it does not authorize advertising, resale, or generalized model training.
These restrictions also apply to transfers to a paired AI service. Only pair
a recipient configured to handle the shared data for your requested task under
these restrictions. We do not grant a recipient permission to use Google data
for unrelated purposes. Agent Gateway has no feature that uploads decrypted
Gmail content to our staff for human review.

4. STORAGE AND RETENTION

On iOS, credentials, private keys, and review state use device-local Keychain
storage configured to be accessible when the device is unlocked and without
iCloud synchronization. The encrypted notification inbox uses a backup-excluded
file with iOS file protection. Previews and the email review screens are
obscured when the app becomes inactive. No storage or transmission method
guarantees absolute security.

Muse-composed send requests, including recipients and message text, are retained
in device secure review storage for review and replay prevention. They follow
the bounded request retention below; closing the review does not delete them.
Encrypted notification requests may remain in the protected inbox until handled.
Decline does not submit mail. Muse independently holds its composed content
under its own retention policy. The app does not save Gmail drafts.
Successful sending creates copies held by Google and the recipients under
their retention settings, outside the app's local cleanup rules. Normal email
delivery does not have the paired-agent channel's endpoint encryption; Gmail
and recipient mail systems process the message for delivery.

Requests expire after a bounded period, generally no more than five minutes.
Saved approval rules last at most 24 hours. Local request and replay records
are pruned according to the app's expiry rules, generally within 24 hours after
request expiry when cleanup runs. The local review audit is capped at 100
events and seven days. Cleanup occurs during app processing or initialization;
closing the app does not guarantee immediate physical deletion of every record.

The relay coordinates live encrypted delivery without a persistent email
message or result mailbox. Active pairing and encrypted destination records
are retained to support the connection until replaced, disabled, or revoked
as applicable. Revoked pairing records and security counters can remain for
replay prevention; the current registry does not assign them a fixed automatic
deletion period. Provider operational records follow the provider's applicable
retention practices. Data received by your paired agent follows that recipient's
retention practices.

5. YOUR CHOICES AND DELETION

You can preview or deny data requests, choose one-time sharing, decline a send request,
reset saved approvals,
disconnect Gmail, unpair Muse, or turn off notifications in the app or iOS
settings. Disconnecting Gmail removes locally stored Gmail credentials and
clears the app's source-review state. The app attempts to revoke Google access;
if that request cannot complete, revoke the grant in your Google Account's
third-party access settings as well:
https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Unpairing removes the local pairing and attempts to revoke the relay pairing
and notification route. A network failure can delay remote cleanup. Previously
shared information remains with the recipient unless that recipient deletes it.
Deleting the app should not be relied on as the only way to revoke Google access
or remove relay records; disconnect and unpair first when possible.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information
held by the operator, contact hello@zerobox.ai. We may need information
to verify your request and identify relevant pairing records. Do not send
passwords, OAuth tokens, private keys, or email content in a support request.
Some security records may need to be retained to prevent replay or meet
applicable legal obligations. We will explain relevant limitations when
responding to a request.

For a deletion request, identify Agent Gateway and the pairing or device record
you want removed if you know it. We can help remove active relay pairing and
notification destination records after appropriate verification. Local Gmail
tokens and plaintext previews are not held by the relay; use Disconnect Gmail
to clear source-review state on the device. Security revocation records have
the retention limitation described above. Contact Google or the paired
recipient separately about information they hold independently.

6. CLIPBOARD AND EXTERNAL SERVICES

When you use setup-copy actions, pairing instructions, a temporary code, or
agent configuration can be written to the system clipboard for you to paste
into your trusted Muse session. Clipboard contents and the destination chat
are outside the app's secure vault and may be retained or synchronized by
your device or the receiving service. Treat a pairing code as confidential.
The app does not automatically paste or send clipboard contents to Muse.
